# Landing page

Scope: `/` marketing landing. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: hackathon judges (live demo, projector/laptop) and recruiters; secondary, distributor quoting reps and industrial buyers.
Action: "Try a sample RFQ" opens the live Workspace on a messy sample in one click. Secondary: paste your own (goes to /app).
Proof: the mechanism shown working (decoded RFQ, held line with question, provenance tags, price receipt). No invented customers, metrics or prices; catalog/prices labelled synthetic. Eval numbers only once real.
Constraints: spec palette and type pinned (paper, ink, teal-steel; Inter UI, JetBrains Mono for specs). Inspiration: scint.ai structure only (workflow walk-through, product UI up top), not its dark look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an engineering drawing sheet of a quote being made: a messy RFQ dimensioned and annotated like a part. It refuses the dark SaaS hero with a floating dashboard screenshot and a row of feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground, ink linework, teal-steel for dimension and leader lines, a drawing frame with zone ticks (A–D, 1–6) on the edges, and a title block in the corner holding run metadata. Archivo semi-condensed display; Inter body; JetBrains Mono for every spec, grade and price. Status in shape, colour and word.
STORY: A visitor sees a real messy RFQ decoded field by field, sees one line held with a specific question instead of a guess, and clicks one option to watch it re-run into the quote. They learn that only one step is AI and every number has a receipt, then try a sample.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width drawing sheet. Headline top-left inside the frame; subcopy and the "Try a sample RFQ" primary action under it. Across the lower two-thirds: the RFQ email specimen left, decoded ledger lines right, joined by leader lines from substrings to spec chips; title block bottom-right.
FORM: Pinned by the brief (spec section 4), no roll. Signature interaction: the held line on the sheet resolves in place when an option is clicked, and the ledger total ticks up.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
